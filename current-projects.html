Active Study

COMPASS-ND Orthostatic Hypotension (OH) Substudy

Lead student: Joshua Andari

Project Overview

Tracking the longitudinal relationships between cardiovascular and cerebrovascular hemodynamics and cognitive change across the cognitive spectrum.

Active Study

VIBE (Vascular and Integrated Brain Effects of Estrogen)

Lead student: Jaclyn Baggley

Project Overview

Examining how estrogen exposure relates to vascular and cerebrovascular function, cognition, and brain health across older adults with diverse hormone and sex/gender profiles.
