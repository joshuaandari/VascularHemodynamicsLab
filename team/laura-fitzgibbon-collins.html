Principal Investigator

Dr. Laura K. Fitzgibbon-Collins

Dr. Laura Fitzgibbon-Collins is the Principal Investigator of the Vascular, Brain and Mobility Lab, also known as the VBM Lab, at Western University’s School of Kinesiology.

Her research examines how vascular and cerebrovascular health relate to mobility, cognition, brain health, and healthy aging across adulthood.

Research Areas

Vascular and Brain Health

Cerebrovascular and cardiovascular function across aging, disease, and everyday physiological change.

Mobility, Cognition and Fall Prevention

How movement, cognition, gait, balance, and falls risk interact across adulthood.

Women’s Health and Inclusive Research

Sex-specific aging, women’s health, hormone-related physiology, and inclusive participant representation.
