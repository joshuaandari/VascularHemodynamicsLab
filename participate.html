Participate in Research

Express interest in current studies with the V.B.M Lab.

We welcome community members who are interested in learning more about research participation opportunities in the Fitzgibbon-Collins Lab at Western University.

Who should use this page

This page is for adults who would like to hear from the lab about current or upcoming research opportunities. Submitting interest does not commit you to a study, and a lab team member may follow up with more information if there is a suitable match.

The lab uses a secure institutional form for any submission.

Current study options

  • Longitudinal changes in cerebrovascular autoregulation during supine-to-stand transitions

These options are manually maintained and can be updated later as active studies change.

Secure intake form

This Microsoft Form is the secure submission destination for participation interest.
